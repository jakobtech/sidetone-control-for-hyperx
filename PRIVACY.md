# Privacy Policy: Sidetone Control for HyperX

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sidetone Control for HyperX does not collect, store, or share personal data.

- **No telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting.**
- **No internet connections.** The app only talks to the HyperX NGENUITY service on this computer
  (localhost) to read the headset status and apply the sidetone level.
- **Local settings only.** The chosen sidetone level is saved in a small text file in the app's
  settings folder on this computer. "Start with Windows" uses the standard Windows startup settings.
  Nothing leaves the computer.

Uninstalling the app removes it. In the direct-download version, you can delete the
`%APPDATA%\SidetoneControl` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SidetoneControl` folders to remove its settings.

Questions: contact the publisher through the Microsoft Store listing.
